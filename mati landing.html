The method

Three moves, in order, every time.

Like winding a tape reel: the sequence is the whole point. Skip a step and the recording doesn't happen.

1
Wind

Set the length of your session — or let Loop pull it from the calendar block you just opened.

2
Run

The dial sweeps while you work. Distracting apps stay locked out until the hand comes back around.

3
Log

The session is saved automatically to your history — no notes to write, nothing to mark complete.
